**CORSAIRDASH: Sovereign Seas** reimagines the fast-paced arcade delivery loop of `DixieDash` across a living **2.5D / 3D Golden Age of Sail Archipelago**. Command your vessel from harbor to harbor, sign wax-sealed Royal Cargo Charters, customize your ship's modular loadout slots across **6 Vessel Tiers**, sing 4 multi-voice Sea Shanties, and either blast through or cleverly outrun swarming pirates and **Legendary Sea Monster Bosses** to claim the ultimate **High Score**!

### 🧩 8 Modular Loadout Upgrades (1 Slot Each • 100% Gold Refund on Unequip!)
- **📦 Expanded Cargo Hold (`120g`)**: `+3 Cargo Crates` per contract (scales payouts massively, `-2% speed`).
- **💣 Broadside Cannon Battery (`150g`)**: `+2 Port/Starboard Guns`, `+22 Cannon Damage`, `-10% Reload Time`.
- **🎯 Swivel Chase Culverins (`220g`)**: 360° auto-targeting deck guns that fire at the nearest pirate or boss every 2.0s!
- **🛡️ Ironclad Oak Plating (`140g`)**: `+95 Max Hull HP` and `-12% incoming damage`.
- **⛵ Silk Studding Sails (`160g`)**: `+11% Top Speed`, `+15% Acceleration`, and `+18% Wind Tacking Efficiency`.
- **⚓ Copper-Sheathed Keel (`130g`)**: `+22% Turn Rate` and `65% Coral Reef Protection`.
- **🔨 Master Carpenter Crew (`260g`)**: Passively repairs `+4.0 Hull HP/sec` while underway at sea.
- **🛢️ Greek Fire Stern Barrels (`200g`)**: Drops explosive burning oil kegs off your stern (`[E]`) to scorch pursuing pirates!

> 💡 **Smuggler vs. Privateer Dilemma**: Slotting **0 offensive gun modules** activates **Unarmed Smuggler Overdrive (`+12% Speed & +25% Charter Payouts`)**—haul massive cargo loads and cleverly outrun pirates and bosses!

## ⚔️ 3 Difficulty Modes (Sailing Physics + Pirate Swarm Escalation)
1. **🟢 MERCHANT BREEZE (Easy • `1.0x Score`)**: Forgiving wind physics & gentle pirate density escalation (`5 ➔ 14` pirates).
2. **🟡 PRIVATEER TRADEWINDS (Medium • `1.5x Score`)**: Authentic wind-angle sailing boosts & steadily growing pirate hunting packs (`9 ➔ 28` pirates).
3. **🔴 CURSED MAELSTROM (Hard • `2.5x Score`)**: Heavy keel momentum, gale currents, lethal reefs, and relentless pirate armada swarms (`15 ➔ 46` concurrent pirates)!

## 🕹️ Controls
| Key / Control | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`W` / `Up Arrow`** | **Hoist Sails** (Accelerate forward; catch crosswinds/tailwinds for speed boost!) |
| **`S` / `Down Arrow`** | **Reef Sails / Backwater** (Slow down & reverse) |
| **`A` / `D` (`Left` / `Right`)** | **Steer Rudder** |
| **`Spacebar`** | **Anchor-Drift Turn** (Sharp broadside pivot!) |
| **`F` / `Left-Click` / `Enter`** | **Fire Port & Starboard Broadsides** |
| **`E`** | **Drop Greek Fire Stern Barrel** |
| **`L` or `B`** | **Open Shipyard, Ship Tiers & Loadout Store** |
| **`R`** | **Toggle 4-Track Sea Shanty Concertina & Crew Choir** |
| **`H`** | **Blow Quarterdeck Brass Foghorn** |
